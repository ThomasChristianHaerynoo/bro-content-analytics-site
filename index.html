Bro Content Analytics

Bro Content Analytics is a small tool that helps the owner of a TikTok account understand how their own videos perform.

How it works

The account owner signs in with TikTok and explicitly authorizes access to their public TikTok videos. The tool then retrieves those videos and generates Excel and PDF reports comparing views, likes, comments, and shares.

Who it is for

The tool is for personal use by the account owner, to analyze their own account. It does not analyze other people's accounts.
